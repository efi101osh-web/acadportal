What We Offer

Everything you need in one place

Built for the University of Calabar — a modern academic management system for one professor and all students.

📚

Lecture Notes

Professor uploads PDFs, DOCX, PPT and more. Students download by level — you only see what's meant for you.

📝

Assignments & Deadlines

Assignments posted with clear deadlines. Submit files or text, re-upload before deadline, and get your score immediately after grading.

🎯

Scores & Feedback

Grades and remarks go live the moment the professor saves them. All scores are archived by course and level.

🔔

Smart Notifications

Get notified in-app whenever a new note or assignment is uploaded for your level. Never miss an update.

🔒

Private & Secure

Students only see their own submissions. No one else can access your work, scores, or profile data.

📱

Mobile Friendly

Fully responsive — works on your phone, tablet, or laptop with a clean modern interface built for students.
